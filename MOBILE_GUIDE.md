## 📲 **Install as Mobile App**

### **On iPhone:**
1. Open GoalQuest in Safari
2. Tap **Share** button (box with arrow)
3. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Enter name: `GoalQuest`
5. Tap **"Add"**
6. ✅ App icon appears on home screen!

### **On Android:**
1. Open GoalQuest in Chrome
2. Tap **⋮** (three dots menu)
3. Tap **"Install app"**
4. Tap **"Install"**
5. ✅ App icon appears on home screen!

**Now you can open GoalQuest like any other app!** 🚀
